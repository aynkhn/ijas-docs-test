# Firearms, Explosives, Rockets, and Projectile Safety
{: .no_toc }

1. TOC
{:toc}

## Why Do We Have These Rules?

Projects involving firearms, explosives, rockets, projectile-launching devices, or other energetic systems present some of the highest risks encountered in student research: serious injury or death from ballistic impact, blast pressure, fragmentation, fire, burns, or unintended launches. These activities require enhanced supervision, specialized facilities, and Safety Review Committee (SRC) approval before experimentation begins.

## Does My Project Involve These Hazards?

- firearms or ammunition;
- explosives or energetic materials;
- pyrotechnics or propellants;
- rockets or rocket motors;
- compressed-gas launchers;
- projectile-launching devices;
- ballistic testing; or
- any device designed to launch an object at high speed.

## Before You Begin Your Project

- obtain prior approval from the SRC before conducting any demonstration or experiment involving firearms or explosives;
- submit a detailed description of the proposed experimental procedure, including the qualified adult who will conduct the experiment;
- allow sufficient time for SRC review and approval before experimentation begins; and
- understand that SRC approval is valid for **30 days** — additional experimentation requires renewed or new approval.

## General Safety Requirements

- wear appropriate eye protection and other required PPE;
- work under the supervision of a responsible adult;
- conduct all demonstrations and experiments in compliance with all applicable local, municipal, state, and federal laws and regulations; and
- immediately stop any activity if unsafe conditions develop.

## Firearm Safety

Students may **not**:

- possess, handle, or utilize any firearm at any time while conducting a science fair demonstration or experiment;
- hand-load or reload ammunition;
- utilize black powder or muzzle-loading firearms;
- modify or alter any firearm for experimental purposes; or
- bring firearms or ammunition to any school, Regional, State, IJAS, ISAS, or ISEF Science Exposition.

{: .important }
> When firearm-related projects are approved, extensive additional requirements apply (qualified 21+ supervisor with specific state credentials, ballistic shield, liability insurance naming IJAS as additional insured, liability releases, and restrictions on firearm age and ammunition type). See the full endorsement documentation before pursuing any firearm-related project.

When firearm-related projects are approved:

- the firearm shall be handled, and the demonstration/experiment conducted, at all times by a person over 21 who is certified as a police officer by the Illinois Law Enforcement Training and Standards Board, or by a person licensed as a Private Detective or Private Security Contractor with a valid Firearms Control Card issued by IDFPR. A Concealed Carry License or PERC card does **not** satisfy this requirement;
- the student and all bystanders shall remain behind a ballistic shield and wear appropriate eye and hearing protection;
- the supervising professional shall provide a signed statement describing the demonstration, certifying it is safe, explaining why, and providing copies of all required credentials;
- the supervising professional shall provide a Certificate of Liability Insurance of at least $1,000,000 naming IJAS as Additional Insured;
- the facility shall provide its own Certificate of Liability Insurance of at least $1,000,000 naming IJAS as Additional Insured;
- the student, parent/guardian, supervising professional, and all persons present shall execute any required liability releases;
- only commercially manufactured firearms less than 50 years old may be used; and
- only commercially manufactured fixed-cartridge ammunition made to SAAMI standards may be used.

## Explosives, Rockets, and Projectile Safety

Students may **not** independently manufacture, modify, or test explosives, explosive mixtures, or improvised energetic materials.

Projects involving rockets, rocket motors, projectile-launching devices, compressed-gas launchers, or other energetic systems must:

- operate only under appropriate adult supervision;
- be conducted only in locations appropriate for safe operation;
- maintain safe distances between operators, observers, and launch areas;
- inspect all equipment before each launch or test;
- follow all manufacturer instructions and applicable safety codes; and
- comply with all applicable local, municipal, state, and federal laws and regulations.

{: .note }
> Model rocketry projects using commercially manufactured motors are exempt from the firearm provisions above, but must be supervised by a parent, guardian, or teacher at least 21 years old and must comply with all applicable laws and recognized model rocket safety practices.

## Documentation

Include in your Safety Sheet:

- a description of all hazards associated with the project;
- the safety procedures used to minimize those hazards;
- required supervision;
- PPE used;
- testing procedures and locations;
- copies of all SRC approvals and supporting documentation; and
- all additional documentation required for firearm-related investigations.

{: .note }
> If uncertain whether your project falls under this section, consult your teacher or Regional Safety Chair before beginning your investigation.
