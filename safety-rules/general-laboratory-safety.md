## General Safety Rules

All students must:

- work under the supervision of a responsible adult;
- wear appropriate personal protective equipment (PPE), such as safety glasses, gloves, or protective clothing, whenever required by the project;
- use equipment only as instructed and for its intended purpose;
- keep work areas clean and organized;
- follow all school, laboratory, local, state, and federal safety rules and regulations; and
- safely store and properly dispose of all chemicals, biological materials, and other hazardous waste.

{: .note }
> Sponsors are responsible for teaching students safe laboratory practices before experimentation begins.

## Safety Assessment

Every project is different. Before you begin, think about the materials, equipment, and procedures you will use. Talk with your sponsor about any possible hazards and how you will prevent accidents. If your sponsor is unsure whether your project can be done safely, they should ask the Regional Safety Chair before beginning your research.

{: .note }
> Students and parents should **not** contact the Regional Safety Chair or State Safety Chair directly.
