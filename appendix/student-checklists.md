## Student Checklist for Experimental Projects

### Abstract

- [ ] First page of paper
- [ ] Three paragraphs with proper headings: Purpose, Procedure, and Conclusion
- [ ] Typed single-spaced 250 words or fewer

### Safety Sheet for Poster Session Only

- [ ] High School students use ISEF forms/ Middle School students use IJAS forms in separate packet next to display board
- [ ] Hazards listed, precautions described
- [ ] Signed by sponsor

### Endorsement(s)/Required Documents, if applicable for Poster Session Only

- [ ] High School students use ISEF forms/ Middle School students use IJAS forms in separate packet next to display board
- [ ] Completed as directed by guidelines
- [ ] Signed by student and sponsor; proper documentation is attached, if necessary

### Title page

- [ ] Clear and concise

### Table of Contents

- [ ] Pagination is present and accurate

### Acknowledgements

- [ ] Credit is given to those who have helped

### Purpose & Hypothesis

- [ ] States precisely what the investigation is attempting to discover or solve
- [ ] States a definitie question or problem
- [ ] Hypothesis is in IF-THEN-BECAUSE format

### Background Research (BR)

- [ ] Consistent voice is evident.
- [ ] Logical and/or related grouping of information
- [ ] Accuracy in calculations, spelling, grammar, and quotations
- [ ] Typed double spaced, 1 inch margins, may be double sided
- [ ] Parenthetically cited (in-text) using APA formatting

### Materials & Methods of Procedure

- [ ] Any apparatus and materials are listed.
- [ ] Step by step chronological procedures are present. Metric measurements are used (SI), if possible.
- [ ] Drawings and photographs are present if they enhance and clarify the procedure.
- [ ] Number of test groups and number of trials within each test group is adequate.
- [ ] Control or comparison group is defined.
- [ ] Independent, dependent, and constant variables are defined.

### Results

- [ ] Data is organized into tables or charts with accompanying graphs.
- [ ] Data is quantitative and correct metric units (SI)  are used
- [ ] Data is sufficient, clear, and accurate.
- [ ] Data is analyzed and discussed.

### Conclusions

- [ ] Evaluation and interpretation of data is present.
- [ ] Refers back to purpose & hypothesis; answers the original question with substantive experimental and BR information.
- [ ] Is valid and limited to the results of the experiment.

### References

- [ ] References come from a variety and type of resources.
- [ ] References are current and all are cited in text.
- [ ] References are alphabetical.
- [ ] Proper APA format is used for all references.

- [ ] First page of paper

### Safety Sheet for Poster Session Only

- [ ] High School students use ISEF forms/ Middle School students use IJAS forms in separate packet next to display board
- [ ] Hazards listed, precautions described
- [ ] Signed by sponsor

### Endorsement(s)/Required Documents, if applicable for Poster Session Only

- [ ] High School students use ISEF forms/ Middle School students use IJAS forms in separate packet next to display board
- [ ] Completed as directed by guidelines
- [ ] Signed by student and sponsor; proper documentation is attached, if necessary

### Title page

- [ ] Clear and Concise

### Table of Contents

- [ ] Pagination is present and accurate

### Acknowledgements

- [ ] Credit is given to those who have helped

### Problem or Need

- [ ] States need being investigated or explains how design will solve a problem

### Background Research (BR)

- [ ] Consistent voice is evident.
- [ ] Logical and/or related grouping of information
- [ ] Accuracy in calculations, spelling, grammar, and quotations
- [ ] Typed double spaced, 1 inch margins, may be double sided
- [ ] Parenthetically cited (in-text) using APA formatting

### Materials & Methods of Procedure

- [ ] Any apparatus and materials are listed.
- [ ] Establish design criteria.
- [ ] Step by step chronological procedures are present. Metric measurements are used (SI), if possible.
- [ ] Drawings and photographs are present if they enhance and clarify the procedure.
- [ ] Description of prototype and redesigns is included.
- [ ] Description of how prototype and redesigns are tested and evaluated.

### Results

- [ ] Results are organized into tables or charts with accompanying graphs if appropriate.
- [ ] Results are quantitative if possible and correct metric units (SI) are used.
- [ ] Results are sufficient, clear, and accurate.
- [ ] Results are analyzed and discussed.

### Conclusions

- [ ] Evaluation and interpretation of results is present.
- [ ] Refers back to problem or needs statement; Explains how redesigns affected performance or results.
- [ ] Is valid and limited to the results of the investigation.

### References

- [ ] References come from a variety and type of resource.
- [ ] References are current and all are cited in text.
- [ ] References are alphabetical.
- [ ] Proper APA format is used for all references.
